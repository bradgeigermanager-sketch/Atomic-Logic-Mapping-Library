### **Component Details & Specifications**

#### **1. Atomic Logical Artifact (ALA) Standard**

Every entry must adhere to the following metadata structure (YAML Front Matter):

* `uid`: Unique identifier (e.g., `ALA-001`).
* `title`: Human-readable name.
* `category`: Taxonomic label for sidebar grouping.
* `accessibility_label`: Screen-reader identifier.

#### **2. The "Three-Pass" Verification Engine**

Your logic flow is formalized into the following functional sequence:

* **Internal Pass:** `check_internal_logs` (Validates temporal/physical proximity).
* **External Pass:** `check_external_datasources` (Validates entity/org credibility).
* **Contextual Pass:** `cross_reference_master_lib` (Maps findings to existing history).

#### **3. Interface Interaction Logic**

* **Search Filter:** Local browser-side filtering of `Index.json`.
* **Content Injection:** Tab-based display (`main` vs `logic`) for immediate context switching.
* **Stability:** Frame-based viewing (`iframe`) to maintain sidebar state during document refresh.
